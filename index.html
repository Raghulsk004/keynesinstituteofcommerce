About Keynes Institute

Keynes Institute of Commerce - Established 1985

Established in 1985, Keynes Institute of Commerce is approved by the Government of Tamil Nadu (Regd. No. 220470). We specialize in Typewriting and Shorthand (English, Tamil & Hindi), providing expert training with individual attention.

With a strong legacy of excellence spanning over four decades, we have successfully trained thousands of students who have secured prestigious positions in government and private sectors. Join us to experience an enriching journey that opens doors to top-tier careers and lifelong success!
